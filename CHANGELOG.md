# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-27

### Features
- First version: `custom:airing-card` and `custom:airing-mini-card` tell whether to air the home now — **Yes**, **Meh** (a short airing), **No** or **No need** — from the rooms' CO₂ and humidity against the outdoor air quality, pollen, rain and temperature.
- Advised airing time (+5 min when CO₂ is very high, 5 min at most when it is cold or not ideal), the reasons as chips, an indoor / outdoor table and a calm tile per room.
- A button times the airing: a `timer` helper when set, else the card counts down by itself.
- Rooms from the config, or from the Smart Comfort integration's room sensors; outdoor readings from any sensors (Atmo France, WAQI, Météo-France…) or from Smart Comfort.
- Illustrated header: a window whose curtains open with the answer, a breeze while it is open, rain and a haze outside; night with the dark theme.
- Visual editor for both cards, light and dark themes, 22 languages.
